The organiser may publish a companion listing from a private plan. Only selected itinerary titles, places, dates and times are copied into its public preview. Tasks, notes, assignments and the identities of existing private members are not published with that preview. Later private edits do not update it automatically. How discovery works

The For you wall ranks visible stories using countries you want to visit, travel-style similarity, saves, freshness and variety. Destination interests and travel style give useful suggestions even before you have an interaction history; older saves have less influence. The app also varies authors and destinations. Following shows recent stories from people and groups you follow. You can switch this filter, search, choose a culture topic, or use the map. Companion listings are ordered by departure date, without a compatibility score. No ranking choice overrides privacy or eligibility rules.
