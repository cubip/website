Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what cubip collects, why, where it is kept, for how long, and what you can do about it. cubip is operated from Germany, so the General Data Protection Regulation applies to everything we do with your data, wherever you live; additional rights may apply under the law where you live, including the UK, United States and Taiwan. Nothing here limits those rights.

Who is responsible. Ichieh Hsu, trading as cubip, Germany, is the controller of your data; the imprint has the postal address. You can reach us at support@cubip.com.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work and nothing else. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDK or advertising profiles, and we never sell or rent your data.

What we collect and why

To sign you in. Your email address, or the relay address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email. There are no passwords and we never ask for a phone number. Purpose: to run your account. Basis: the contract between us (these terms). If you do not give an email address or an Apple ID, you cannot have an account.

Your profile. Your display name, your home city (the city only, not an address), your birth year, your travel style, and the countries you have visited and wish to visit. Purpose: to show your public profile to other signed-in users subject to blocks, and to personalise the home wall. Your birth year is used to display age. Story audience controls apply to stories, not to the whole profile. Basis: the contract. Your gender is optional. It is used only to apply the gender restriction an organiser may put on a trip post, so that people who do not match never see the post. Basis: your consent, which you may withdraw by selecting the option not to disclose it. If you do not give it, you will not be shown posts that carry a restriction.

What you share. Your travel records (place, dates, a few lines of text and an optional journal), the photographs you choose to add to them, your trip posts, your applications to other people's trips, your invitations, and your messages. Purpose: to publish them to the people you have chosen, to run applications and conversations, and to act on reports. Basis: the contract, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

Safety. Reports you make and blocks you set, reports others make about you, and the decisions we take on them. Purpose: to keep people safe and to enforce the terms of use. Basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service, and our legal obligations.

Identity verification, if you choose it. A photograph of an identity document. It goes into a private store that no part of the app can read, is looked at once by a person at cubip to confirm that it matches your profile, and is deleted by an automatic daily job within a day of that review. If nobody has reviewed it fourteen days after you sent it, the job deletes it anyway, the request lapses, and you can send a new photograph. After that we keep only the fact that you were verified and the date. Purpose: to let you post trips and to show a verified mark. Basis: your consent. Verification also affects access to stories whose authors limit them to verified people and eligibility for companion applications.

Technical data. Our hosting provider records the time and network address of requests to keep the service running and to detect abuse; we do not use this data for anything else. The app itself sends no analytics, crash reports or device identifiers to us or to anyone.

We do not collect your location from the device, your contacts, your full photo library unless you separately enable the optional visited-country scan, or anything from other apps. The optional photo-library scan processes dates and locations on your device to suggest visited countries; it does not upload the library. Records use places you enter and map coordinates resolved when saved. Planning searches send the text and area or route endpoints you choose to Apple Maps; cubip does not request device GPS access.

Photographs

Before a photograph leaves your phone, the app removes its GPS position and device metadata. There is no setting for this; it always happens. Photographs are stored privately and served through links that expire within an hour, and anyone who obtains an already-issued link may use it until it expires. Changing visibility or blocking prevents new authorised access, but cannot recall downloaded copies or immediately revoke existing links. For each record you choose who can see it: everyone on cubip, verified people, or people you have travelled with.

Who sees your data

Other users see what you have chosen to show them: your profile, your records at the visibility you set, your trip posts, your applications to the organiser you sent them to, and your messages in conversations you are part of. A trip post with a gender restriction is shown only to people who match it. Direct and group conversations, and private trip plans, require an accepted request or invitation. Accepted members can read earlier messages and the shared plan. An accepted companion application joins the same plan and chat.

cubip sees what is needed to run the service and to act on reports, including reported messages and identity documents under review.

Providers provide the following services. Supabase and Resend process service data on our instructions under their applicable data-processing terms; Apple also processes data independently under its own policy:

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in. The servers are in Frankfurt, Germany, in the European Union.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, the App Store, place search, maps and transport estimates. Apple receives the queries, places and technical information needed for those requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers the email that carries your sign-in code. It sees your email address and that email only.

We do not give your data to anyone else, except where the law requires it, to respond to a court order or a lawful request from an authority, to establish or defend a legal claim, or where we believe in good faith that it is needed to protect someone from serious harm. If the service is ever transferred to a successor, your data goes with it under this policy, and we will tell you.

Groups, follows and planning

Social activity: we store likes, tags, individual and group follows, accepted group membership, invitations and follow-suppression choices to provide the social features you request (contract). Following a group follows existing accepted members and future joiners; former-member follows remain. An individual unfollow, follower removal or block prevents automatic re-following. Pending group invitations are private; accepted membership and public group content are visible to other users subject to access rules.

Private planning: plan titles, destinations, dates, itinerary places and times, notes, tasks, assignments and revisions are visible to accepted members of the attached conversation (contract). The organiser can publish selected itinerary fields to a companion listing after reviewing them. This snapshot excludes private notes, tasks, assignments and existing friends’ identities. Accepted companions can then access the existing plan and conversation history. A private participant can remain counted without a public identity; accepting a public companion invitation makes their party membership visible.

Shared contributions and deletion: leaving a group or plan removes access but does not undo text already contributed to someone else’s story or plan. Such writing can remain without an account attribution where needed to preserve the other participants’ shared content (legitimate interest), subject to your rights and removal requests. A group owner’s account deletion removes the group and detaches other contributors’ stories. The original contributor’s account deletion removes their own stories. Plans owned by a deleted account are removed; other people’s plans remain. Account deletion removes uploaded photos and identity documents through a server process before deleting the account. Failed deletion can be retried; contact support if it cannot finish.

Personalised discovery

The app uses wishlist countries, travel styles, saves, freshness and variety to rank visible stories on the For you wall. This is personalisation, not advertising, and it does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. You can choose Following for recent stories from followed people and groups, use search or culture topics, or browse the map. Your written content is not automatically translated when you change interface language.

Where it is kept and international transfers

Your data is stored and administered in Germany: the servers are in Frankfurt and cubip operates from Germany. It leaves the EU only through Apple and Resend, which process data in the United States; those transfers rest on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in the provider's data processing terms. If you live in the United States or in Taiwan, your data is stored in the EU rather than where you are, and you reach it from wherever you use the app.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and content: while your account exists. When you delete your account, they are removed at once, and any backup copies expire under the hosting provider’s configured backup retention period; they are not used for ordinary access. If a backup is restored, deletion requests must be reapplied.
  • Identity documents: deleted automatically within a day after the review, and at the latest fourteen days after you sent them, whether or not the account is later deleted. The verified mark and its date stay for the life of the account.
  • Messages: while the conversation exists. When you delete your account, the messages you sent are removed from every conversation.
  • Reports, blocks and moderation decisions: while the accounts involved exist, and longer if a claim or an investigation is open. A report you made stays after you delete your account, without your name attached, so that it can still be acted on.
  • Request logs at our hosting provider: kept for the configured operational and security retention period, then removed or aggregated. We can provide the applicable period on request.
  • Email you send us: as long as needed to answer it and to show what was agreed, then deleted.

We keep data longer only where the law requires it or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • see and correct your data in the app, or ask us for a copy of everything we hold about you;
  • delete your account from Settings in the app, which removes your account and your content;
  • withdraw consent to the optional things: choose not to disclose your gender, or ask us not to keep the verified mark;
  • choose what to show: every record has its own visibility, and your profile shows only what you put on it;
  • object to processing that rests on our legitimate interests, and ask us to stop, restrict, or move your data to you in a portable form;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: the data protection authority of the German state where cubip is based, or of the EEA country you live in; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in the United States, your state's attorney general; in Taiwan, the competent authority under the Personal Data Protection Act.

Write to support@cubip.com from the email address on your account. We respond without undue delay and within one month for GDPR requests; where a lawful extension or another applicable deadline is needed, we explain it within the initial response period, and we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. There is no charge, and we never treat you differently for exercising a right. If we refuse a request, we tell you why, and you may appeal by replying to that email; a person reviews the appeal and answers within 45 days.

If you live in the United States

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, do not use it for targeted advertising, and do not use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. There is therefore nothing to opt out of, and we do not respond to browser opt-out signals because there is no sale or sharing to stop. The only sensitive personal information we process is the identity document you may choose to send for verification, and gender if you choose to give it; both are used only as this policy says, and never for inferring anything about you. The categories we collect, the purposes, and the recipients are listed above, and we have collected only those in the twelve months before the date at the top of this policy. You may exercise the rights listed above yourself or through an authorised agent who can show they act for you.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens live in the device's keychain. Data is protected in the database by row-level rules that decide, for every row, who may read or write it; the app cannot get around them. Photographs and documents are in private storage. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

cubip is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data. If you believe a child has an account, write to support@cubip.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When the change matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
